Frequently asked questions about legal advice in Qatar

How do I contact a lawyer at Al Murqab Law Office?

+

Call or WhatsApp us on 7190 0190, email info@almurqablaw.com, or visit our office on Salwa Road in Doha, Sunday to Thursday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Urgent consultations are available 24/7.

What areas of law does Al Murqab Law Office handle?

+

We advise and act in criminal, civil, commercial, corporate, administrative, real estate, labour and family law, as well as arbitration and dispute resolution — for individuals and companies alike.

Do you advise companies and foreign investors in Qatar?

+

Yes. We advise companies on formation, structuring, contract drafting, compliance and shareholder disputes, and help non-Qatari investors understand the rules on investing and setting up a business in Qatar.

Can I get legal advice in English?

+

Yes. We provide consultations, correspondence and legal representation in both Arabic and English, and regularly work with expatriates and foreign companies in Qatar.

What should I bring to my first legal consultation?

+

Bring copies of the documents relevant to your matter — contracts, correspondence, notices and any earlier judgments or decisions — with your ID and a short timeline of events. This lets the lawyer assess your position accurately from the first meeting.

Is what I tell a lawyer confidential?

+

Yes. Everything a client shares is subject to complete professional confidentiality — a legal and ethical obligation we uphold on every matter.

How are legal fees agreed?

+

Fees depend on the nature, complexity and stage of the matter, and are agreed with the client in writing before work begins, so the position is clear from the outset.
