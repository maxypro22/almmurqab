You may need a labour lawyer in Qatar when your salary is late, your employment is terminated without a lawful reason, or your end-of-service gratuity is withheld. Employers need one too: when facing a labour complaint, or when they want contracts and internal policies that will stand up to scrutiny. At Al Murqab Law Office we act for employees and employers alike, and we approach every file the same way: what Qatari labour law provides, and what the documents can prove.

Labour disputes in Qatar follow a set route. A claim is first submitted to the Ministry of Labour for an attempt at amicable settlement, and is referred to a Labour Dispute Settlement Committee if no agreement is reached. Calculating entitlements accurately and framing the right claims from the outset makes a real difference. Before contacting us, you may find it useful to read our guides to end-of-service gratuity in Qatar and filing a labour complaint.

We advise in Arabic and English, and we keep employees' personal data and employers' business information in complete professional confidence, whether the client is an individual employee or an establishment with a large workforce.

The legal framework a labour lawyer in Qatar works within

  • The Labour Law (Law No. 14 of 2004), as amended: including Decree-Law No. 18 of 2020, which reorganised notice periods.
  • Law No. 13 of 2017: which established the Labour Dispute Settlement Committees, each chaired by a judge of the Court of First Instance.
  • Law No. 17 of 2020: which sets the minimum wage for workers and domestic workers of all nationalities.

Employee rights in Qatar: the questions we hear most

End-of-service gratuity
At least three weeks' wage for each year of service for employees with one year or more, calculated on the last basic wage, with fractions of a year paid pro rata (Article 54).
Notice after probation
One month during the first two years of service and two months thereafter; a party who fails to give notice must pay the other the wage for the notice period or its unexpired part.
Minimum wage
QAR 1,000 basic monthly wage, plus QAR 300 for food and QAR 500 for accommodation unless the employer provides them.

Dismissal without notice or gratuity

Article 61 sets out the only cases in which an employer may dismiss an employee without notice and without end-of-service gratuity. They include assuming a false identity or submitting forged certificates or documents, committing an error that causes the employer gross financial loss (provided the employer notifies the competent department within 24 hours of becoming aware of it), and disclosing the establishment's secrets. Outside these cases, an employee does not lose notice or gratuity, and the dismissal may be challenged as unfair; see our guide to unfair dismissal in Qatar.

How a labour complaint is handled

  1. The claim is submitted to the Ministry of Labour through its electronic channels or offices, with supporting documents.
  2. The Ministry attempts an amicable settlement.
  3. If settlement fails, the dispute is referred to a Labour Dispute Settlement Committee.
  4. The Committee's decision may be appealed to the Court of Appeal within the statutory time limit, and is then enforced.

For employers

Review your employment contracts and internal policies regularly to keep them in line with the Labour Law as amended, and see our guide to notice periods and resignation.

Frequently asked questions

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated in Qatar?

+

An employee with one year or more of service is entitled to gratuity of at least three weeks' wage for each year, with fractions of a year paid pro rata. It is calculated on the last basic wage, and the employer may deduct any amount the employee owes the establishment.

What notice period applies on resignation or termination?

+

After probation, notice is one month during the first two years of service and two months thereafter. A party who does not observe the notice period must compensate the other with the wage for the notice period or the part not worked.

Can I take a labour claim straight to court in Qatar?

+

No. A labour dispute is first submitted to the Ministry of Labour for amicable settlement and, if that fails, is referred to a Labour Dispute Settlement Committee. The Committee's decision can then be appealed to the Court of Appeal.

What is the minimum wage in Qatar?

+

Law No. 17 of 2020 sets a minimum basic wage of QAR 1,000 per month, plus QAR 300 for food and QAR 500 for accommodation unless the employer provides them. It applies to workers and domestic workers of all nationalities.

Can I be dismissed without end-of-service gratuity?

+

Only in the cases listed in Article 61 of the Labour Law, such as submitting forged documents, disclosing business secrets or a serious error meeting the statutory conditions. If none of those cases applies, the employee can challenge the decision and claim their entitlements.

Do you also act for employers?

+

Yes. We act for both employees and employers, and for employers we draft contracts and internal policies and respond to complaints before the Ministry and the Labour Dispute Settlement Committee. We do not act for opposing parties in the same dispute.
