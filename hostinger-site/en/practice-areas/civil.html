People usually turn to a civil litigation lawyer in Qatar once amicable solutions have failed: a contractor who did not finish the job, a borrower who did not repay, an accident that caused injury or damage, or a contract whose terms the parties read differently. At that point the real questions are threefold. Does the right exist in law? What evidence supports it? And is the claim still within the limitation periods set by the Civil Code?

At Al Murqab Law Office we begin with a frank assessment of how strong the claim is and what it will demand in time and effort, then choose the right route: a formal notice, a negotiated written settlement, or a carefully prepared lawsuit. We handle compensation claims in Qatar arising from tortious and contractual liability, unjust enrichment and recovery of payments made without entitlement, construction disputes, and money claims and debt recovery between individuals.

Our work does not end with the judgment. A judgment that is never enforced does not return what you are owed, so we follow enforcement proceedings before the Enforcement Court until the award is collected. The legal framework for civil claims in Qatar

The Civil Code promulgated by Law No. 22 of 2004 is the core source of the rules on contracts, liability for wrongful acts, unjust enrichment and limitation. The procedure for filing a claim, conducting the case and appealing judgments is governed by the Civil and Commercial Procedures Law promulgated by Law No. 13 of 1990, as amended. After judgment, the Judicial Enforcement Law No. 4 of 2024, in force since November 2024, applies and created a dedicated Enforcement Court.

Annulment of a contract3 years depending on the ground, and in all cases 15 years from conclusion of the contractArticle 161
Unjust enrichment3 years from knowledge of the right to compensation, or 15 years from when the right arose, whichever is soonerCivil Code

Two points worth noting

Where a compensation claim arises from a crime, it does not become time-barred while the criminal proceedings continue, even if the periods above have passed (Article 219). Limitation is counted in days, not hours, and if the last day falls on an official holiday the period extends to the next working day (Article 409).

Deadlines for appealing civil judgments

The period to appeal a civil judgment is thirty days, or twenty days in urgent matters, unless the law provides otherwise (Article 164 of the Civil and Commercial Procedures Law). A further challenge to the Court of Cassation is available in the cases and under the procedures set out in Law No. 12 of 2005 on cassation challenges in non-criminal matters.

Common compensation claims in Qatar and civil actions

Compensation for harm

A tort claim rests on three elements: fault, harm and a causal link between them. The claimant bears the burden of proving the harm and its extent, and the court frequently appoints an expert to assess it, so the quality of medical, technical and financial evidence directly affects the amount awarded.

Contract disputes

Where one party breaches a contract, the other may, depending on the circumstances, seek performance or termination with damages. We always start with the contract and the parties' correspondence, because notice clauses, penalty clauses and performance periods often decide the direction of a dispute before it reaches court.

Enforcing judgments and instruments

Enforcement requests are now handled by the Enforcement Court, with procedures increasingly carried out electronically. The Judicial Enforcement Law gives cheques the force of an enforceable instrument, and gives registered or notarised leases that force for eviction after the lease expires, which shortens the path for a number of claims.

What to prepare before meeting a civil litigation lawyer in Qatar

  • The contract, its annexes and all related correspondence.
  • Invoices, receipts and bank transfers proving the sums claimed.
  • Medical reports, police records or technical reports relating to the harm.
  • Photographs and digital evidence with their dates.
  • A timeline showing when the harm occurred and when you learned of it and of the person responsible, as this sets the start of the limitation period.
  • Any notices you sent or received, and any earlier judgments or decisions in the same dispute.

Frequently asked questions

When do I need a civil litigation lawyer in Qatar?

+

You need a civil litigation lawyer in Qatar when someone else's act has caused you loss, when a party breaches a contract with you, or when a money claim is filed against you. A lawyer helps you assess the strength of your position, calculate limitation periods and choose between settlement and litigation. They also pursue enforcement after judgment until the sum is recovered.

What is the limitation period for compensation claims in Qatar?

+

A claim for liability arising from a wrongful act lapses three years from the day the injured party learned of the harm and of the person liable, or fifteen years from the act, whichever is sooner (Article 219 of the Civil Code). If the claim arises from a crime, it does not lapse while the criminal proceedings continue. Claims for personal rights generally lapse after fifteen years unless the law sets a different period.

What is the deadline to appeal a civil judgment in Qatar?

+

The appeal period in civil matters is thirty days, or twenty days in urgent matters, unless the law provides otherwise, under Article 164 of the Civil and Commercial Procedures Law. A cassation challenge may then be available in the cases defined by Law No. 12 of 2005.

How is compensation assessed in a civil claim?

+

The court assesses compensation according to the harm proven before it and its link to the act that caused it, and the claimant bears the burden of proving the harm and its extent. Courts frequently appoint an expert to assess losses, so accurate medical, technical and financial evidence is decisive.

Can I claim compensation if the incident was a crime?

+

Yes. The injured party may join the criminal case as a civil claimant or bring a separate compensation claim before the civil court, depending on the circumstances. A compensation claim arising from a crime does not lapse while the criminal proceedings are ongoing, even if the ordinary limitation periods have passed.

What happens after a judgment is issued in my favour?

+

An enforcement request is filed with the Enforcement Court created by the Judicial Enforcement Law No. 4 of 2024, which decides enforcement requests and related disputes. The law gives enforcement judges wider powers to speed up recovery, and your lawyer follows the process until the awarded sums are paid.
